- Lessons:
  - **Gradients tint button text:** a UIGradient on a TextButton also tints its text (a red X on a red gradient disappears). Put glyphs in a child label.
  - **Module cache in Edit:** require() of the same module in Edit returns the cached version. Clone it next to its siblings to get fresh code.
  - **Mouth angle:** "w" mouth pieces angled the wrong way read as a frown. The inward-down slant reads as a smile.
  - **Almond eyes:** angled almond eyes read as angry. Use soft ovals for friendly elemental creatures.
  - **Rarity rings must stay on the ground.** A ring that rides with a hovering model reads as a floating disc.
