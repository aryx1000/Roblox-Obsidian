- Lessons:
  - Roblox **Glass hides transparent parts behind it**: use opaque fluid/bubbles or a SmoothPlastic shell.
  - A **Top-face SurfaceGui** lays text along the part's Z axis.
  - With **StreamingEnabled**, client raycasts and descendant scans at startup are unreliable: use known heights and `DescendantAdded`.
  - `LocalTransparencyModifier` doesn't hold on non-character parts; a client-side `Transparency` write does.
  - Disabling StarterGui ScreenGuis for Edit-mode screenshots is risky: record and restore them, as was done here.
