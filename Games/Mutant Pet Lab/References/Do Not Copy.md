# Do Not Copy

Check this **alongside** the positive references before any design. Each row says where the rule comes from:
- **Approved:** a [[Design Decisions]] entry. Binding.
- **Owner feedback:** your own words. Binding.
- **Research:** a recommendation.

## Styles and art you explicitly dislike
| Don't | Source |
|---|---|
| Creatures that look like **primitive Roblox parts assembled together** (sphere + cylinder ears, obvious stacking) | Owner feedback 2026-10-16 · Approved |
| Species told apart **mostly by colour**; a sphere with accessories | Approved (creature style rule) |
| **Whole-body glow**; Neon as the creature's identity | Approved (glow rule) |
| Rarity as "same creature but brighter" | Approved |
| Tiny dot eyes; the same eyes on every species; an *angry* low-wellbeing face | Approved (face language) |
| Humanoid plant NPC look for Mossbit; thin rabbit body or upright biped for Frostpup | Owner feedback (C selections) |
| Copying any reference's exact art, characters, names, fonts, wood/parchment frames or branding | Approved (reference rules) |
| **A generic futuristic lab** (chrome/white sci-fi boxes, cyberpunk neon) for buildings | Owner instruction 2026-10-20 ("Do NOT design a generic futuristic lab") |
| ~~Smooth "balloon" bodies when faceted is wanted~~ → **dropped 2026-10-20:** faceting is selective (frost/crystal/geological/prismatic/selected heroes); soft mammals, plants, gels and fluffy creatures stay soft. Don't facet them automatically | Approved (owner 2026-10-20) |
