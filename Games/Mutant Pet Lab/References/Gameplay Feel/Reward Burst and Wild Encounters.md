# Reward Burst and Wild Encounters

> [!info] Inspiration from research, not a decision
> These are official promo art from other games, kept for private reference only. Never reuse them in the game.

## Images
![[research-bgsi-reward-burst.png]]
*Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity. The reward is a glossy, sparkling object with a short number, and the creature has a delighted expression.*

![[research-pet-catchers-wild-catch.png]]
*Pet Catchers. Wild creatures roam the world, and catching one is a shared, visible moment.*

## What I like
- The reward appears as a **physical, glossy object** with one short number ("+1M"), not a text line.
- The creature reacts to the reward (sparkle eyes).
- Wild creatures in the world give a reason to explore, and other players can see them.

## What I do NOT want copied
- Meme faces, inflated trillion-scale numbers, "CLICK TO CATCH" prompts.

## Apply this to MUTANT PET LAB
- A bio-assay collect becomes a glowing sample vial that arcs to the HUD with a short "+3", and the creature does a happy bounce.
- A candidate for later: a roaming "wild specimen" sighting in a biome. See [[Simulator Feature Research]].
