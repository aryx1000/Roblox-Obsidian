## What I like
- **Bioluminescence as identity:** emissive fungi and crystals against dark bases. This fits a mutant-lab valley.
- Accent light in a few saturated colours, everything else muted.
- Future areas you can see from the hub.

## What I do NOT want copied
- Their exact assets or layout.
- Night-only darkness everywhere (cozy direction).

## Apply this to MUTANT PET LAB
- Biome edges (map step F): add a few emissive fungus or crystal clusters per biome in the biome's accent colour, within the part budget (Neon material or PointLights used sparingly).
- Long-term candidate: a visible, locked area (for example the dormant Expedition Arch) lit as a "next goal".
