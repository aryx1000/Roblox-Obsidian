## What I like
- **The silhouette carries the species.** Colour only distinguishes variants.
- Idle "life" behaviour: sleeping, bouncing, looking around.
- Large, expressive eyes for readability on phones.
- Size as an obvious rarity or growth signal.
- Colour slots per region: a possible model for mutation colour inheritance.

## What I do NOT want copied
- Exact characters or IP.
- One body plan for every species (the PS99 pattern); that is exactly MPL's current risk.

## Apply this to MUTANT PET LAB
- Group the 36 [[Species]] into about 6 body plans, one per egg family, each with a clearly different silhouette. Vary features inside a family.
- Give every creature at least one idle-life animation (sleep, eat, react).
- Mutation results could take region colours from each parent.

Shape first, then face, then colour. A creature is a pet when it sleeps, eats and reacts.
