## What I like
- **Escalation by changing the whole palette and scene**, not just a bigger number.
- A short info card: name, rarity, one key value.
- Silhouettes for undiscovered or secret entries (matches the [[Index Collection Screen]] reference).

## What I do NOT want copied
- "1 in 800,000,000" gambling framing. MPL should show **rarity tiers**, not slot-machine odds, as the hero element.
- Long unskippable cutscenes.
- Their art, typography or logos.

## Apply this to MUTANT PET LAB
- Normal hatch: no takeover. Rare: a tinted glow. First discovery: a lab "NEW SPECIES" stamp. Secret: the reactor lights the sky in the creature's colour. Full proposal in [[Egg Hatching Research#Recommended MPL hatch flow]].
- The reveal card shows species name → rarity colour → trait/variant chips.
