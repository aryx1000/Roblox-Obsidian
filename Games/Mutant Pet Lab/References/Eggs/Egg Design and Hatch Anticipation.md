## What I like
- **The egg predicts the creature:** its shape, pattern and colour hint at the family.
- **Cracks with light coming through** are the anticipation beat.
- The rare tier has its own unmistakable surface (rainbow or galaxy).
- The egg sits in a **nest or incubator that belongs to the world**.
- Growth stages keep one visual identity.

## What I do NOT want copied
- Exact designs, characters, logos or thumbnail text.
- Meme-style thumbnail composition (red arrows, "YOU!").

## Apply this to MUTANT PET LAB
- Each of the six [[Egg Families]] gets an egg silhouette and pattern that hints at its family.
- Incubators show glowing cracks in the last seconds before HATCH. Mutation green → purple light through the cracks fits the approved accent ([[Design Decisions#UI skin: friendly research device (lab skin, reference structure)]]).
