# Map Overhaul

## Approved direction (2026-10-15)
> [!success] Approved — see [[Design Decisions]]
> - **Goal:** a research ranch built into an interesting **mutant valley**, not flat grass with decorations.
> - **Composition:** elevation, terraces, slopes, raised/lowered campus sections, rock formations, vegetation masses, retaining structures, sight lines, terrain around plots. Build areas stay usable and predictable.
> - **Landmark:** the **Mutation Reactor** at the centre of the campus plaza — visible from every property, green/purple glow, chunky silhouette, pipes/tanks/machinery at its base, not so large it blocks the map.
> - **Also:** campus plaza, paths, plot surroundings, biome edges, lighting (Phase 1 step F).
> - **Frozen:** `PlotNN` origins and bounds, station interaction points within the 40-stud checks.

### Key facts from the audit
- Ground is one flat 1060 × 1060 slab; 8 plots sit on a ring (r 255) and occupy **r 217–362** at full expansion — frozen and flat.
- Code depends on the Plots, the 4 station `InteractButton`s, `Campus/EggFacility/EggShopCounter` and the `OpenResearchPrompt`. Landscape, BioWetlands, ValleyRim, the core landmark, `CreatureDisplayArea` and `Baseplate` have **no script references**.
- Reactor must reach **y ≥ 46** to be seen from every plot (Plot05's view is blocked by the 24-stud expedition arch) → spec: core y 50, crown y 56.
- Elevation is added **around** gameplay floors (campus y 1, plots y 1, paths y 0.6), never under them; parts-based so the backup can capture it.

**Built:**
- **Lighting:** 14:30 warm afternoon, haze 1.2, restrained bloom (threshold 1.6), and `MPL_ColorCorrection` for phones. The old values are in `map_overhaul/lighting_baseline_2026-10-15.json`.
- **Mutation Reactor** (`MutantWorld/MutationReactor`, 68 parts): green → purple core, glass shell, crown to y 56, 4 coolant tanks, pipes, 2 catwalks, cables to the stations. The neon parts are named for later animation.
- **Research Terminal** moved to the reactor front at (0, 3, 21). Prompt, logic and server authority are unchanged.
- **Plaza and wayfinding** (`Campus/Promenade`, `Campus/Wayfinding`): promenade ring, benches, colour lanes to every station, low signposts, 8 plot markers and a reserved discovery-board plinth.
- **Specimen Showcase** (`Campus/SpecimenShowcase`): dais, frame, header and walkway. Pedestal rings use rarity colours. No new logic.
- **Valley:** campus-edge wall, commons berms + lane banks, plot surround walls/rocks/shrubs, and 2 outer terrace tiers. 48 trees were re-seated onto the terraces.
- **Biomes:** 8 habitat zones, one per gap, each with its own materials, hero props and a seam rock blending toward its neighbour.
- **Performance:** flags off on 452 legacy decor parts. Workspace has 1,949 instances (≤ 2,000). The Baseplate was retired to `ServerStorage/MapOverhaul_Backup`.

**Deviations** (**approved 2026-10-16** as implementation notes in [[Design Decisions#Map overhaul implementation (approved implementation notes)]]):
- Round dais.
- 2 terrace tiers instead of 3.
- Berms run as radial ridges beside the lanes, because pools and plot fences sit where the ring berms were planned.
- No lowered wetland basin.
- Wider wall apron, because the plot fences reach |X| 74.5.
- Biome ridges sit on the gap axis.
- Lighting `Technology` was left unchanged (not reachable from MCP).
- The reactor is hidden from inside the south station buildings.
