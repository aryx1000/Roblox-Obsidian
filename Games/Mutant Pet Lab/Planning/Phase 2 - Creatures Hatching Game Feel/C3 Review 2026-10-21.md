### Systemic layout rules added
1. **Guide tracker = "notice band".** It lives between the HUD pod's right edge and the right rail's left edge, measured live. Too narrow for the full card → condensed (step + title); too narrow for that → hidden. **While any major panel is open it yields entirely**, so rails and close buttons always win. It never uses the bottom/thumb zones. (`OnboardingController`)
2. **Left rail safe area.** The rail starts right under the HUD pod; on touch screens, if one column would enter the thumbstick reserve (bottom ~24%), it wraps into **two columns of full-size 56 px buttons** instead of shrinking tap targets. (`ProgressionHUDController.applyRailWidth`)
