## Checkpoint 2: creature refinements
- **Pebbletoad:** curious/friendly default eyes (round pupils, bigger glints, a second sparkle) instead of sleepy.
- **Magmawisp:** softer face (lower muzzle patch, bigger round eyes, centred pupils, small smile): magical, not angry.
- **Sparkbug / animator:** one `BulkMoveTo` per rig, LOD-hidden parts skipped, wing sides hinged as one unit, slower/smaller flap at medium LOD, no blinks beyond near. **Sparkbug 34.8 → 20.3 µs/step (near), 33.8 → 18.2 µs (medium); Frostpup 26.2 → 19.1 µs.** 24 Sparkbug-class worst case ≈ 0.49 ms (budget 0.5 ms).
- **Rarity marker:** now a soft tinted shadow under the body (no Neon disc), still grounded under flyers/wisps.
