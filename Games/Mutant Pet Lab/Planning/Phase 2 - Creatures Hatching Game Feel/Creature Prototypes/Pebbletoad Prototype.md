## Silhouette
Low, wide toad: two big hind-leg haunches, bulging eyes on top, flat stone plates on the back.

## Proportions
Body 2.8 wide × 1.5 tall — wider than tall is the hopper read. Hind legs 1.0 × 1.0 × 1.7. Eyes 0.72 on raised bumps. Height ≈ 2.3.

## Colour
Olive skin (128, 140, 88); cream belly (214, 204, 150); grey slate plates; green Neon spots (150, 255, 120).

## Material
SmoothPlastic skin; Slate plates.

## Signature anatomy
**Huge hind legs + bulging eyes** (wide mouth line under them).

## Mutant feature
**Glowing moss spots** between the plates (Role Glow).

## Animation implications
Hopper plan: almost still at idle, then big hops (0.6 studs) when moving; idle bob is clamped so it never sinks.

## Lineage
Mutation result of Rockling + Mossbit — **stone plates from Rockling, moss patches from Mossbit**, per the inheritance table. Parent of Sporephant (stone-heavy legs).
