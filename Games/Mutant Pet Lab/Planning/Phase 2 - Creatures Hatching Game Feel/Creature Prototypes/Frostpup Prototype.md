## Silhouette
Four-legged pup: big round head forward, long ears, a short tail up at the back and a ridge of three spikes along the spine.

## Proportions
Body 1.9 × 1.6 × 2.6; head 1.8 × 1.6 × 1.6 (head nearly as big as the body = baby proportions); legs 1.0 tall; height ≈ 3.6 with ears. Rare scale ×1.1 in game.
