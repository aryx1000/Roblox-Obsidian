## Silhouette
Wide gel dome sitting on a flat puddle skirt; two eye stalks rise from the top. Reads as a 'blob with periscope eyes' in black silhouette.

## Proportions
Body 2.8 × 2.0 × 2.6 studs; skirt 3.3 wide (wider than the body, so it pools); total height ≈ 3.1 with eyes. Eyes are 0.62 — large for the body (cute).

## Colour
Lime gel (110, 220, 120), 22% transparent; nucleus (200, 255, 110) Neon; mouth dark green.

## Material
SmoothPlastic with slight reflectance for wetness; Neon nucleus.

## Signature anatomy
**Eye stalks** (two thin cylinders, tilted outward).

## Mutant feature
**Neon nucleus** visible through the translucent gel (Role Glow, pulses for Luminous).

## Animation implications
Gel plan: big squash-style bob (0.12 at 2 Hz), bouncy move (0.22). Drips are Accent (hue drift for Chromatic). Later: stretch on hop, eye stalks lag behind turns.
