# Economy

Three currencies: **Cash**, **Research**, **XP** (Level is derived from XP, Levels 1–25). Full audit, unlock map and simulation: [[ECONOMY_2C]].

## Core loop
Grow crops → feed creatures in pens (wellbeing, favourite food, habitat) → collect bio-assay samples (**3 Cash / 4 Research / 1 XP** each) → buy [[Egg Families|eggs]], [[Research Tree|research]], upgrades and land → breed [[Mutation Recipes|mutations]] → fill the [[Species]] Index.

## Specimen Supply
Free starter specimens from the campus supply: 8 s cooldown, costs 10 Cash, gives 5 Research, cycles Mossbit → Frostpup → Embercub → Slimelet → Rockling → Sparkbug. Server range check 40 studs (Checkpoint E). Cycle and cooldown are session-only ([[Persistence]]).

## Sources and sinks (summary)
| Sources | Sinks |
|---|---|
| Bio-assays (repeatable) | Eggs 60–750 Cash (repeatable) |
| Guide 18 steps, 22 assignments (one-time) | Research tree 830 R total |
| First discovery 15 C / 20 R / 50 XP per species | Pen upgrades, 8 lab upgrades, land Stages 2–4 |
| | Mutations (Research, repeatable, max 300) |

## Pacing (simulated optimal play — lower bounds)
Level 5 ≈ 11 min · Stage 2 ≈ 20 min · Unstable Egg ≈ 40 min · Stage 4 ≈ 97 min · Level 25 ≈ 158 min.

## Decisions
- 2026-10-13: assay payouts cut from 6/8/6 to 3/4/1 (Level 25 was reachable in ~35 min).
- 2026-10-14: **no new post-Level-25 sink** in Build B (Checkpoint E). Revisit with [[Monetization TODO]] / a later build.

Lessons: [[Economy and Progression Design]].
