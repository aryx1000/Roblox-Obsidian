## TOP 10: things similar games do that MPL should consider
1. **Hybrids that visibly combine both parents** (Slime Rancher largos), so every mutation reads as A + B. Planned; make it non-negotiable for the 36.
2. **Physical egg pedestals with proximity odds** (owner R5/R8, Build a Zoo, Steal An Egg): locked eggs dark and chained, unlocked with a price plate.
3. **Player-triggered crack** (owner R4) inside the approved tiered hatch.
4. **Intensity scales with egg/rarity** (owner R9): darkened backdrop and a 3-line stat card for top tiers.
5. **Index family pages with completion rewards** (owner R1; MSM, Dragon City books).
6. **Plot visiting and showing off** (MSM likes, Grow a Garden, Adopt Me): MPL's weakest dimension.
7. **Visible care reactions**: eat, happy, sleep, sad (Adopt Me, Slime Rancher), using the approved face language.
8. **Samples as physical drops** in the pen before collecting (Slime Rancher plorts).
9. **Strong, readable variants** (Adopt Me Neon, Build a Zoo mutations): MPL's are subtle.
10. **A creature template sheet + showcase pipeline** (owner R12/R13), so every species is designed on paper and reviewed in motion.

## TOP 10: things MPL should keep unique
1. **The lab/science fantasy**: research tree, Mutation Lab and **Reactor**. No comparator scores above 1 on Research/Lab.
2. **Recipes that create new species** (30 recipes) instead of duplicate fusion.
3. **Secret mutations with progressive hints.**
4. **The central shared Reactor** as a world-reacting reveal stage.
5. **Creatures that live in pens you walk through**, not icons in a menu.
6. **Calm, non-PvP ranch** (no stealing, raids or predators).
7. **"Cute collectible organism first, mutant biology second"** (approved style).
8. **Server-authoritative fairness**: no paid luck, speed or auto-hatch.
9. **Care that matters** (wellbeing, favourite food, habitats) without punishing absence.
10. **One-screen-at-a-time, side-rail UI** that keeps the world visible.

## TOP 10: things NOT to copy
Full list in [[Do Not Copy]]. Headlines:
1. Paid speed/luck/auto-hatch upgrades (the contents of owner R7's hex board).
2. Weather/event-only mutations (weather isn't approved).
3. Purchase-based pity systems.
4. Limited-time FOMO eggs/countdowns and strike-through "was" prices.
5. Multi-day timers with premium skips (Dragon City, DragonVale).
6. Stealing/raids (Steal a Brainrot, Steal An Egg).
7. 200+ near-identical recolours (breadth over depth).
8. Trading economies (not approved).
9. Wild visitors/sightings (not approved).
10. Number-spam HUDs and stacked boost timers.
