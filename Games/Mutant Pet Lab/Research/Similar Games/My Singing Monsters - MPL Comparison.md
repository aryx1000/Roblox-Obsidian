## Compared with MPL
- **They do better:** **Every monster adds a voice to the island's song**: the collection is audible as well as visible; islands are visited and liked by others; rare variants are clearly framed as 'Rare'.
- **MPL does better:** Lab/science fantasy, mutation secrets, live 3D creatures you walk among.
- **What MPL should learn / adapt:** Creatures as **ambient life**: each body plan has a small idle sound/loop so a full ranch *sounds* alive (fits the sound review gate). Plot visits with a like/rating.
- **Avoid:** Paying to skip breeding; long incubation; inventory/currency sprawl.
