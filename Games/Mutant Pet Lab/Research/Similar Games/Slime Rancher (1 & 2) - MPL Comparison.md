# Slime Rancher (1 & 2) (similarity 21/30)

**Why it is similar:** Catch slimes, keep them in corrals, feed them crops/meat they like; they produce **plorts** (a resource). Feeding a slime another type's plort creates a **largo**, a visible hybrid of both.

## Compared with MPL
- **They do better:** **Hybrids look like both parents at once** (the largo is the clearest 'mutation reads as A + B' example in games). Creatures produce a **physical** resource you pick up. Every slime has a personality that's obvious from its face and movement.
- **MPL does better:** Eggs and hatching, secret recipes, Roblox multiplayer hub, an index of 36 named species.
- **What MPL should learn / adapt:** (1) Mutation results visually combine parents (already in the Visual Language spec; Slime Rancher proves it lands). (2) Samples as **physical drops** in the pen (bio-assay vials) before collection. (3) Favourite food doubles output (MPL already does this; make the reaction visible).
- **Avoid:** Chaos events that destroy ranches (MPL is calm); first-person suction gameplay.
