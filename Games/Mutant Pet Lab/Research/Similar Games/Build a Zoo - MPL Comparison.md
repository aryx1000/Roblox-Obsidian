# Build a Zoo (similarity 20/30)
> Research-level only ([[Claude Workflow#Authority order]]). Not played first-hand; confidence: **medium (official description; breeding unconfirmed)**. Nothing here is a decision.

**Why it is similar:** A hatching tycoon: buy eggs from a conveyor, place them on your **zoo plot**, animals hatch and earn; expand tiles; **mutations** (Gold, Diamond, Electric, Fire, Jurassic, Aurora) change look and value; weather events apply mutations; fishing for ocean eggs.

## Compared with MPL
- **They do better:** **Eggs physically placed on the plot** hatch where they sit; mutations are **very visible** (material/colour + aura); chunky voxel art that looks premium; a pity system guarantees a rare mutation.
- **MPL does better:** Breeding/mutation recipes, research, discovery, care/wellbeing, a central hub.
- **What MPL should learn / adapt:** Variants should read as strongly as Build a Zoo's mutations (MPL Luminous/Chromatic/Prismatic are subtler). **Free** pity for secrets (e.g. failed secret attempts reveal more of the hint, already a candidate).
- **Avoid:** **Weather mutations** (weather isn't approved), a purchase-based pity system, seasons/premium eggs, an egg conveyor treadmill.
