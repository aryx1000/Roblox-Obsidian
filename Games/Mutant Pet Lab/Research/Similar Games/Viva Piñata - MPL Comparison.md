## Compared with MPL
- **They do better:** **Creature requirements as puzzles** that shape how your plot looks; each species has its own house; the garden is the collection.
- **MPL does better:** Direct hatching and mutation, readable progression, multiplayer.
- **What MPL should learn / adapt:** Habitat preferences that visibly shape the pen (MPL habitats exist); species-specific pen props (a Gel likes a puddle, a Sprout likes soil).
- **Avoid:** Wild visitors/sightings (**not approved**), predation, opaque requirement lists, breeding minigames.
