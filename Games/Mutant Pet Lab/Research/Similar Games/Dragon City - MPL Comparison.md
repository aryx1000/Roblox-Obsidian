## Compared with MPL
- **They do better:** Dozens of parallel breeding slots and a Dragon Book that shows every combination's odds; feeding visibly **levels** creatures (growth); habitats double as income buildings.
- **MPL does better:** MPL's creatures physically live and move in a 3D pen you walk through; secret recipes with hints; no paid skips; a shared central hub (the Reactor).
- **What MPL should learn / adapt:** Make habitat matching **visible** (a happy/sad reaction when a creature is in its preferred habitat); a breeding 'Dragon Book' style recipe log in the Index (MPL already shows known recipes).
- **Avoid:** Timers in hours/days with gem skips; energy systems; event-only dragons; hundreds of near-identical recolours.
