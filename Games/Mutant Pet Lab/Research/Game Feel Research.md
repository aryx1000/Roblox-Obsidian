# Game Feel Research

> Authority: REFERENCE-level research. Evidence for game feel is thin. Most sources describe mechanics, not feel, so most points here are **INF**. First-hand observation would be the best next step. The safe way to get it is short clips the owner captures; Claude must not open or test other experiences in Studio.

## Patterns
- **Physical carry/delivery.** The egg in your arms in [[Steal An Egg]]; characters walking to your base in [[Steal a Brainrot]]. (OBS)
- **A small forgiving skill moment before a reward.** The Fisch reel: "okay to mess up". (OBS)
- **Growth you can watch.** Swarm motion in [[Bee Swarm Simulator]]; plants in [[Grow a Garden]]. (INF)
- **Constant burst feedback.** Coins and numbers popping. Strong at first, noise later ([[Pet Simulator 99]]). (INF)
- **Day/night rhythm and light** carrying emotion ([[99 Nights in the Forest]]). (INF)
- **Low-stakes calm** described as the appeal ([[Grow a Garden]], press). (OBS)

## For MPL (candidates only)
1. **Every core action gets a small physical response:**
   - Planting: a seed pop.
   - Harvest: the crop flies to you.
   - Collect: the sample vial arcs to the HUD.
   - Mutation: the reactor pulse.

   The UI kit's `Sounds` slots (Click, Open, Close, Place, Error, Pickup, Rotate) are still empty. Fill them first; it is cheap and high-impact.
2. **Creatures react:** a happy bounce on favourite food, and a look at the player when nearby.
3. **Keep bursts short.** No screen-filling number spam.
4. **Optional skill moment** (see [[Fisch]]), never required.

## Hatch and mutation feel (pass 2)
See [[Egg Hatching Research]] and [[Mutation Reveal Research]].
- DevForum sentiment (S38): hatches that are **too fast to read** feel cheap. Add a **light burst** on opening, sound, and 2D particles.
- Repeated long animations annoy players. Use rarity-scaled length, short versions for owned species, and tap to skip (S46, S47).
- Light leaking through cracks is the anticipation beat ([[Egg Design and Hatch Anticipation]]).
- Rewards as glossy objects with one short number, and creatures reacting ([[Reward Burst and Wild Encounters]]).

## What not to copy
- Tension from the threat of losing progress.
- Paid speed-ups to the "feel" moments.
