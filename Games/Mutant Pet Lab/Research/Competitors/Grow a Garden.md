# Grow a Garden

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** Released 2025-03-26 by BMWLux; co-owned by Splitting Point Studios (Jandel). It peaked at roughly 21–22M concurrent players in mid-2025 and was the fastest game to 1B visits, in 33 days ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S9]]).
- **INF:** Its crop → sell → better seeds loop, plus mutations, is the closest *farming* match to MPL's crop beds → feeding → samples loop.

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** You get an empty plot and a little money. Buy carrot seeds → plant → harvest → sell → buy better seeds ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S9, S10]]).
- **OBS:** Crops keep growing while you are offline ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S9, S10]]).
- **INF:**
  - First 30 s: a seed in the ground.
  - First 2 min: the first harvest and sale.
  - First 5 min: a second seed type.
  - First 15 min: a mutation seen on a crop, or a restock you waited for.

## UI observations
- **INF (not verified on screen this pass):** Few panels: seed shop, sell, garden. Most state can be read from the plants themselves.

## Map/environment observations
- **INF:** Players' plots sit next to each other, so you can see other gardens and judge their progress at a glance. Social comparison happens without any UI.

## Creature/pet observations
- **OBS:** Pets come from eggs bought in a shop ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S9]]).
- **OBS:** Mutations (weather, sprinklers) change how crops look and raise their value substantially ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S10]]).

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** The seed shop is **global**, so every player sees the same stock. It restocks every 5 minutes, and limited items arrive weekly ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S9, S10]]).
- **INF:** A shared restock gives friends something to talk about ("rare seed in stock!") and a reason to come back. It is a timer that feels social rather than a punishment.

## Game-feel observations
- **OBS:** Players and journalists describe it as low-stakes and laid-back: you can just hang out ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S10]]).

## What works
- The first action is obvious and reversible (plant a seed).
- Visible growth: the world changes because of you.
- **Mutations are visual and valuable at the same time.** That is MPL's exact theme.
- Offline progress makes coming back feel good.

## What I would avoid
- **Community sentiment / press, multiple independent sources:**
  - Players called events "rushed and not very inspired," and the developer acknowledged it publicly ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S11]]).
  - The press described thin variety (NME, via S11).
  - DoBig Studios was criticised for excessive monetisation ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S9]]).
  - Kotaku said it looked more like a prototype than a finished game (S9).
- Robux to steal other players' crops ([[Research Sources#Grow a Garden|S9]]).

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** A mutation should be **visible on the creature** and change its value. MPL already has Luminous/Chromatic/Prismatic ([[Traits and Variants]]); the [[Creature Overhaul]] should make them unmistakable.
- **MPL:** Consider a *shared* rotating egg/specimen offer as a gentle return reason, with no FOMO pricing. Candidate only.
- **MPL:** Updates need real content, not reskins. Plan fewer, richer updates.
