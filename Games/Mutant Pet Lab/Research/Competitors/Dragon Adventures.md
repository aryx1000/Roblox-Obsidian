# Dragon Adventures

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** Made by Sonar Studios. Over 200 dragon species; you hatch, raise and breed them and design a lair. Won a 2020 Bloxy Award for Favorite Map ([[Research Sources#Dragon Adventures|S29, S30]]).
- **INF:** The strongest Roblox example of **creature breeding + home base + themed worlds**, which is MPL's whole premise. Map quality is part of its reputation.

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** Find eggs in nests across each biome → hatch → raise from birth ([[Research Sources#Dragon Adventures|S29, S31]]).

## UI observations
- No source analysed the UI.

## Map/environment observations
- **OBS:** Themed worlds (fire, ice, nature, shadow and others) with eggs placed in the world ([[Research Sources#Dragon Adventures|S30]]).
- **OBS:** A Favorite Map award ([[Research Sources#Dragon Adventures|S29]]).
- **INF:** Each world is a strong single theme: one colour family and one material family, read at a glance.

## Creature/pet observations
- **OBS:** Dragons grow from hatchling to "massive" ([[Research Sources#Dragon Adventures|S29]]).
- **OBS:** Bred offspring inherit traits from both parents, including element, stat spread and colour mutations ([[Research Sources#Dragon Adventures|S30]]; third-party guide).
- **INF:** Size progression makes growth obvious, and inherited colour makes every bred creature *yours*.

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** Growth → breeding → lineages ([[Research Sources#Dragon Adventures|S30]]).

## Game-feel observations
- No source.

## What works
- **Inheritance you can see.** A child looks like its parents.
- Themed worlds with an unmistakable identity.
- A lair you customise as the home for your collection.

## What I would avoid
- Over 200 species: a big content load. MPL has 36 and should go deep before wide.

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** In [[Mutation Recipes|mutation]] results, show what came from each parent: colour from one, pattern or material from the other. Candidate for the [[Creature Overhaul]].
- **MPL:** The planned biomes should each be *one strong theme*. This matches the approved "one biome per gap, organic transitions" decision.
