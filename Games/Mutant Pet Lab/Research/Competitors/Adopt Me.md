# Adopt Me!

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** Made by Uplift Games and released 2017-07-13; peaked at 1.92M concurrent players; over 40.8B visits by November 2025 ([[Research Sources#Adopt Me!|S12]]).
- **OBS:** #3 on the 2026-10-15 chart, about 255K concurrent players ([[Research Sources#Market charts|S1]]).
- **INF:** The longest-lived *pet-bonding* game on Roblox. It shows how care and growth hold players for years.

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** The starter egg hatches only a dog or a cat. Caring for pet needs (eating, drinking, etc.) earns Bucks ([[Research Sources#Adopt Me!|S12]]).
- **INF:** The first minutes are about *your* pet following you around. Attachment comes before economy.

## UI observations
- **INF (not verified this pass):** A task/needs list drives the session: "your pet is hungry" gives a clear, small next action.

## Map/environment observations
- **INF:** A town hub with homes and themed destinations. Needs send you to places (school, campsite), so the map is part of the care loop.

## Creature/pet observations
- **OBS:** Pets grow through visible stages: newborn → junior → pre-teen → teen → post-teen → full-grown ([[Research Sources#Adopt Me!|S12]]).
- **OBS:** 4 full-grown pets of one kind combine into a **Neon**; 4 Neons combine into a Mega-Neon ([[Research Sources#Adopt Me!|S12]]).
- **OBS:** Five rarity tiers ([[Research Sources#Adopt Me!|S12]]).
- **INF:** Neon is a glow you can see from across the map. Status and progress are readable *on the creature*.

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** Care actions → age → combine. Trading is a large part of the social life ([[Research Sources#Adopt Me!|S12]]).

## Game-feel observations
- **INF:** A pet that follows you is the "feel" anchor; companionship is the reward.

## What works
- **Growth stages you can see.**
- A combine-up tier (Neon) that is pure visual prestige.
- Needs as small next goals.

## What I would avoid
- **OBS / press:**
  - Rare pets sold for real money off-platform fed a scam subculture targeting children, mainly "trust trades" ([[Research Sources#Adopt Me!|S12]]).
  - In November 2020 the developers added trade licences, trade limits, trade history and unfair-trade alerts (S12).
  - Reports of high child spending (S12).
- Any open player-to-player trading in MPL needs heavy safeguards. Out of scope for now.

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** A [[Creature Overhaul]] candidate: grow-up stages (juvenile → adult) and a visible prestige tier. MPL's variants could work like Neon: a glow/material you can see from anywhere.
- **MPL:** Use creature needs (hungry, favourite food) as the *visible* next goal. MPL already has wellbeing and favourite food ([[Economy]]); surface them on the creature, not only in panels.
