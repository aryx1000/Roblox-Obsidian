# Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

> [!warning] Low evidence
> No source described the hatch animation or the UI. The notes below are mostly mechanics.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** Made by Rumble Studios and released 2025-04-11; quickly became one of the most-played games ([[Research Sources#Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity|S14]]).
- **INF:** A modern egg-hatching simulator from 2025 that shows how the egg loop is packaged today.

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** Hatching is the main way to get pets. Most eggs cost Coins and a few cost Robux ([[Research Sources#Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity|S14]]).

## UI observations
- No source.

## Map/environment observations
- **INF:** Worlds and islands stacked vertically (bubble/float theme). Not verified.

## Creature/pet observations
- **OBS:** Luck and Speed potions affect hatching ([[Research Sources#Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity|S14]]).
- **OBS:** Updates add egg-related rewards such as Rift Shards from hatching ([[Research Sources#Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity|S15]]).

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** A paid Triple Egg Hatch exists ([[Research Sources#Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity|S14]]).
- **INF:** Hatch *volume* is the monetised lever, which pushes hatching toward a slot machine.

## Game-feel observations
- No source.

## What works
- **INF:** Updates plug into the existing hatch loop (new eggs, new rewards) rather than adding new systems.

## What I would avoid
- Paid hatch multipliers and luck potions tied to Robux: the hatch becomes gambling-adjacent. This conflicts with [[Design Decisions#No monetization without explicit approval]].

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** One hatch should be a *moment*, not a batch of 3. Quality over quantity, which suits MPL's slow incubation.
