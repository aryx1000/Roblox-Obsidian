# Ride A Pet

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** #24 on the 2026-10-15 chart ([[Research Sources#Market charts|S1]]).
- **INF:** The pet is a *tool you use* (a mount), not only a collectible. It is a current example of giving creatures a physical role.

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** Buy a radar → find an egg → carry it to a nest on your plot before its break timer runs out → hatch → ride faster → find rarer eggs ([[Research Sources#Ride A Pet|S24, S25]]).

## UI observations
- **INF:** A radar points at eggs, so the world is the goal marker.

## Map/environment observations
- **OBS:** A giant tree holds a physical "ride with a friend" station; servers hold 6 players ([[Research Sources#Ride A Pet|S26]]).
- **INF:** A social feature is placed *in the world* as a landmark instead of a menu.

## Creature/pet observations
- **OBS:** Each pet has four stats: hatch chance, speed, jump and income ([[Research Sources#Ride A Pet|S27]]).
- **INF:** Creature stats are felt directly through movement.

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** 7 rebirths, each needing a specific pet plus a cash target ([[Research Sources#Ride A Pet|S28]]).

## Game-feel observations
- **INF:** Speed is the reward you can feel.

## What works
- **A creature's stats change how you play.**
- Social features as physical stations.
- An egg break timer creates a gentle urgency.

## What I would avoid
- Rebirth targets that grow into the billions (grind).

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** Let a creature's traits show up *in the world*: Luminous lights the pen, a favourite creature follows you around the plot. Candidate for the [[Creature Overhaul]]. No new systems this phase.
- **MPL:** Social features (visiting, gifting) could be physical places on the campus. Future idea only.
