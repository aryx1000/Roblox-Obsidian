## Why this game matters
- **INF:** A long-running creature game whose identity is the **creature design itself**: many distinct, strange species. It is a reference for how a "weird creatures" game presents its creatures.

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** Pick a creature, start as a juvenile, eat and survive, and grow into an adult; growth takes about 26–35 minutes depending on species ([[Research Sources#Creatures of Sonaria|S32, S33]]).

## Creature/pet observations
- **OBS:** Clear life stages from juvenile to adult ([[Research Sources#Creatures of Sonaria|S32]]).
- **OBS:** A 2026 collaboration dragon grows only when players clear corruption from the map. Growth is tied to an action, not just time ([[Research Sources#Creatures of Sonaria|S33]]).
- **OBS:** The wiki mentions choosing, painting and possibly mutating your creature ([[Research Sources#Creatures of Sonaria|S32]]).

## Progression observations
- **INF:** Growth *is* the progression. Nothing is abstracted into a level bar.

## What works
- Growth tied to doing something with the creature.
- Species designs strange enough to be memorable (INF, general reputation).

## What I would avoid
- Survival pressure and creature death, which don't fit cozy.

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** Species should be told apart by **silhouette**, not only by colour. Today MPL species are defined by colour + material + descriptors ([[Creature Overhaul#What exists today]]). This is the strongest creature gap found.
