# 99 Nights in the Forest

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** Made by Grandma's Favourite Games. Peaked at 14.2M concurrent players, about 26B visits by April 2026. Won Best Adventure and Best Horror at the 2025 Roblox Innovation Awards ([[Research Sources#99 Nights in the Forest|S17, S18]]).
- **OBS:** #7 on the 2026-10-15 chart ([[Research Sources#Market charts|S1]]).
- **INF:** Different genre, but the best current example of **one central landmark (the campfire) organising the whole map and loop**. That is directly relevant to the approved [[Design Decisions#Map landmark: the Mutation Reactor|Mutation Reactor]].

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** Rescue four trapped kids within 99 nights. Wolves and bears guard them. Outside the campfire's safe zone at night, monsters attack, and cultists raid the camp every few nights ([[Research Sources#99 Nights in the Forest|S19]]).
- **INF:** Day = go out and gather; night = come back to the fire. A rhythm you understand without a tutorial.

## UI observations
- No source analysed the UI.

## Map/environment observations
- **OBS:** The campfire is the safe zone and base ([[Research Sources#99 Nights in the Forest|S19]]).
- **INF:** Light defines space. The lit area is home and the dark is the unknown. A landmark with a *function* (safety, upgrade) rather than decoration.

## Creature/pet observations
- Not applicable. Creatures here are threats.

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** You build up the camp; seasonal events add content ([[Research Sources#99 Nights in the Forest|S18, S19]]).

## Game-feel observations
- **INF:** Co-op tension and the day/night rhythm. Lighting does much of the emotional work.

## What works
- **A central landmark that matters to the gameplay.** You return to it, upgrade it and gather around it.
- Lighting as wayfinding.

## What I would avoid
- Horror and threat tone. MPL is cozy; take the *structure*, not the mood.

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** The Mutation Reactor should be the place players *return to* and see change: it glows brighter as research progresses, or pulses when a mutation completes. This is a candidate; the approved plan has it as a visual landmark only.
- **MPL:** Use glow from the reactor and path lights as wayfinding. This is already part of the map plan's lighting pass ([[MAP_OVERHAUL_PLAN]]).
