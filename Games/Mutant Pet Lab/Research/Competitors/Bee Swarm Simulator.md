# Bee Swarm Simulator

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **INF:** A long-running creature-collection simulator (developer **Onett**, verified in pass 2 via the official listing, S39; the 2018 date is general knowledge). Its creatures *are* the production engine, and quests from NPC bears guide players through the map. It is the closest structural match to MPL's species → samples → research loop.

## First-session loop
- **INF:** Get a hive and your first bees → collect pollen in the starter field → convert it to honey at the hive → buy more bees and tools.
- **OBS:** NPC bears give quests that point you toward new fields, pollen colours, mobs and rewards ([[Research Sources#Bee Swarm Simulator|S16]]).

## UI observations
- **INF:** The quest tracker is the main guidance UI. NPC dialogue gives each quest a character.

## Map/environment observations
- **OBS:** 23 fields, each with its own flower layout, route, gate and farming role ([[Research Sources#Bee Swarm Simulator|S16]]).
- **OBS:** Gates are tied to hive size; for example, Onett's area needs 30 bees ([[Research Sources#Bee Swarm Simulator|S16]]).
- **INF:** Each field has a *job* (colour, yield), so the map is a tool rather than decoration.

## Creature/pet observations
- **INF:** Bees are shown both as hive slots (collection) and as a visible swarm following you (presence). The collection is always on screen.

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** Quest chains span several fields and escalate a lot; Onett's Star Journey requires billions of pollen ([[Research Sources#Bee Swarm Simulator|S16]]).
- **OBS:** Most quests are optional, but a few chains are required ([[Research Sources#Bee Swarm Simulator|S16]]).

## Game-feel observations
- **INF:** Watching the swarm sweep a field is satisfying: progress shows up as motion and colour, not only numbers.

## What works
- **Map areas with functional identities**, gated by collection size.
- Quest-giver characters make goals feel personal.
- The collection is always visible in the world.

## What I would avoid
- Late-game quest numbers so large they turn into grind. Guides list totals in the billions.

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** Give each planned biome ([[Map Overhaul]]) a *function*. Ideas: habitat-matching boosts, or a specific crop that grows best there. This is a candidate; the map plan currently treats biomes as visual only.
- **MPL:** Station NPCs could voice the [[OnboardingConfig.luau|onboarding]] and assignment steps. Candidate.
