# Sol's RNG

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** Made by Sol's Studio. You roll for 400+ auras of varying rarity, craft gear from them, and drink potions to boost luck (official description, [[Research Sources#Pass 2: hatching and simulator systems (2026-10-15)|S39]]).
- **INF:** The purest example on Roblox of **rarity-reveal presentation**. It is the reference for how reveals escalate, and for what to avoid (gambling framing).

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** Click Roll → reveal an aura. Rare results play a cutscene and may be announced to the server (S46).

## UI observations
- **OBS:** Settings offer thresholds:
  - Cutscene-skip rarity (low tiers don't animate).
  - Auto-equip rarity.
  - Delete/swap warnings above a tier.
  - Aura filter priority.
  - A server-SFX toggle (S46).
- **INF:** Players control how much spectacle they see. Commons never waste time.

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** Collected auras feed crafting (S39).

## Game-feel observations
- **OBS (promo images):** Each rare tier is a different colour world with a title + odds line ([[Rarity Reveal Escalation]]).

## What works
- **Spectacle scales with rarity.**
- **Player-set skip thresholds.**
- Duplicates feed crafting.

## What I would avoid
- Odds ("1 in 800,000,000") as the hero element, and luck potions. Gambling-adjacent; see [[Anti-Patterns]].

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
