# Steal a Brainrot

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** Developer SpyderSammy; owned by DoBig Studios; released 2025-05-16 ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S5]]).
- **OBS:** It holds the Roblox concurrency record: about 25.4M in October 2025, and Guinness lists 25.8M ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S5, S6]]).
- **OBS:** Still #10 on the 2026-10-15 chart ([[Research Sources#Market charts|S1]]).
- **INF:** It is the clearest case study for the "watch your collection earn money" loop.

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** You spawn with a little money and a large storage area, with rival bases all around. A central **conveyor** keeps offering characters. Characters you buy **walk** to your base and earn money every few seconds; rarer ones earn far more ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S7]]).
- **OBS:** Kotaku's reviewer was robbing an AFK player's base within the first minutes. After about 40 minutes they felt they had seen everything ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S7]]).

## UI observations
- **OBS:** No source analyses the UI. The reviewer called the overall look ugly and boring ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S7]]).
- **INF:** The conveyor *is* the shop. Buying happens in the world, so a store panel is barely needed.

## Map/environment observations
- **OBS:** Bases are arranged around a central conveyor. Rival bases are always in view ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S7]]).
- **INF:** A central attraction with bases around it is the same layout as MPL's campus and plot ring. The centre is where the new content appears.

## Creature/pet observations
- **OBS:** More than 25 meme characters in about 7 rarity tiers ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S8]]).
- **INF:** Characters physically walking to your base turns a purchase into a little parade: a delivery moment rather than an inventory line.

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** Rebirth resets progress for better stats. "Admin abuse" events add exclusive characters ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S5]]).

## Game-feel observations
- **OBS:** The reviewer found raiding an unguarded base very satisfying, and losing everything to one lapse of attention very frustrating ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S7]]).

## What works
- New content shows up in a **shared, central, moving** feed that everyone watches.
- Purchases arrive with a physical delivery moment.
- Income can be seen in the world.

## What I would avoid
- **Community sentiment / press, multiple independent sources:**
  - Pay-to-win criticism: Polygon (via [[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S5]]).
  - Paying Robux to shorten lockouts, which Kotaku called cruel monetization ([[Research Sources#Steal a Brainrot|S7]]).
  - Cheaters using auto-collect scripts (Game Rant, via S5).
  - Viral clips of upset children who lost their characters (S5).
- Shallow content: the reviewer had seen it all in about 40 minutes.
- Loss-of-progress as the main source of tension.

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** The [[Map Overhaul|Mutation Reactor]] plaza could be where new things *appear*, giving the centre a reason to visit beyond menus. This is a candidate, not a decision.
- **MPL:** Give a hatched or bought creature a short walk from the incubator to its pen instead of an instant teleport.
- **MPL:** Never make loss or theft a core mechanic. Cozy direction.
