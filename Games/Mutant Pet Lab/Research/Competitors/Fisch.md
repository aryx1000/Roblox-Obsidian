# Fisch

> Legend: **OBS** = observed in a cited source · **INF** = my inference · **MPL** = recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB (not a decision). Community sentiment is labelled as such.

## Why this game matters
- **OBS:** Released 2024-10-05 ([[Research Sources#Fisch|S20]]).
- **OBS:** #17 on the 2026-10-15 chart; the similar *Fish It!* is #15 ([[Research Sources#Market charts|S1]]).
- **INF:** A collection game where *getting* each item needs a small, forgiving skill action. It is a model for making collection feel active rather than idle.

## First-session loop
- **OBS:** Start on Moosewood island and fish. At Level 10 you can buy boats and sail to other islands; 10 islands at launch ([[Research Sources#Fisch|S21]]).
- **OBS:** Reeling minigame: hold or release to keep a bar inside a moving zone while progress fills. The official description says it is fine to mess up a few times ([[Research Sources#Fisch|S20, S22]]).

## UI observations
- **INF:** During a catch, the minigame bar is the only UI. Everything else gets out of the way.

## Map/environment observations
- **OBS:** Islands with distinct catches; some spots are known as farming spots ([[Research Sources#Fisch|S23]]).
- **INF:** Each island has a clear identity and catch table, and you can see it across the water before you can go there.

## Creature/pet observations
- **INF:** Every catch is shown off at the moment of catching (a short reveal).

## Progression observations
- **OBS:** Level → boat → new islands → new fish ([[Research Sources#Fisch|S21]]).

## Game-feel observations
- **OBS:** The minigame is forgiving by design ([[Research Sources#Fisch|S20]]).

## What works
- A **small, forgiving skill moment** before each reward.
- Areas you can see from the start but only reach later.

## What I would avoid
- **INF:** Reeling the same way thousands of times can get repetitive. No source quantified this.

## Lessons relevant to MUTANT PET LAB
- **MPL:** A light, optional, forgiving interaction could make collecting samples or finishing a mutation feel active. Example: a short "stabilise the reaction" tap. Candidate for the later game-feel phase; never required, never punishing.
