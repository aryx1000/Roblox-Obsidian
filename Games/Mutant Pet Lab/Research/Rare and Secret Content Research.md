## Spectrum of information
1. **Fully known** (Adopt Me Neon): no mystery, no frustration, effort-based.
2. **Known to exist, odds published** (BGSI, Sol's RNG): lottery excitement; gambling-adjacent when paired with paid luck.
3. **Silhouette / "???"** (PS99 tease, MPL Index): curiosity with a goal.
4. **Hint only** (MPL secret recipes): experimentation.
5. **Unknown** until someone finds it: community discovery, and frustration if there is no path.

## MPL today
- Eggs never produce Legendaries or secret Mythics (mutation-only). **Top rarity requires play and knowledge, not luck.** Strong design; keep it.
- Normal recipes are shown once both parents are discovered. The 4 secrets show a **hint**. A failed secret attempt is free and returns category feedback + the hint. The Index shows "???" for unknowns, and egg odds per family with "???" names.
- Variants (Luminous / Chromatic / Prismatic) are the luck layer, and the Unstable egg has a variant boost.

**Assessment:** MPL already sits at the healthy middle (levels 3–4) with a free-failure safety net. That is better balanced than most of the examples.

## Recommendations (candidates)
1. **Progressive hints:** each failed secret attempt (free) reveals one more piece of the hint, e.g. "needs a thriving creature" → "a *Radiant* trait helps". Rewards experimentation, avoids wiki-only knowledge.
2. **Silhouettes in the Index** for undiscovered species (the reference already shows this pattern), with the egg family or "Mutation only" printed under them.
3. **Guaranteed fallback** for variants: e.g. after N hatches of a family, the next has a boosted variant chance (pity). Avoids the BGSI 1-in-billions feel.
4. **No limited-time-only secrets.** Seasonal content can return.
5. **Secret = reactor sky event** (see [[Mutation Reveal Research]]). The rarest moment belongs to the world.
