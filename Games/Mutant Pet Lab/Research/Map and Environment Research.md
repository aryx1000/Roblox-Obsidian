## Recommended for MPL (candidates only)
1. **Give each biome a gameplay tie-in**, e.g. creatures whose habitat matches the nearest biome get a small wellbeing boost, or a biome hosts a wild "specimen sighting". Needs design and owner approval. The map work itself stays visual.
2. **A reactor that reacts.** Purely visual hooks to existing events (mutation done, discovery) as a later step after F.
3. **The Specimen Showcase** (approved) can show the server's rarest discoveries: social proof in the world. Candidate.

## What not to copy
- Horror tone, PvP raid zones, speed-gated stealing.
- Huge world lists that read as menus ([[Pet Simulator 99]], INF).
