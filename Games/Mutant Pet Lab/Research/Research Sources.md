# Research Sources

All sources for the [[Roblox Market Research|2026-10-15 research pass]]. Accessed 2026-10-15 unless a publish date is given.

**Labels used in every research note:**
- **OBS:** observed in one of these sources.
- **INF:** Claude's inference.
- **MPL:** a recommendation for MUTANT PET LAB. Never a decision: see [[Design Decisions#Candidates to confirm (not approved decisions)]].
- **Community sentiment:** recorded only where several independent people or outlets say the same thing.

**Reliability:**
- **A:** encyclopaedic or official.
- **B:** named press or an established guide site.
- **C:** fan wiki or guide aggregator.
- **D:** low quality. Used only for mechanics that other sources corroborate.

Many search results were SEO filler pages that copy each other. Those were **excluded**.

**What was not done:** no gameplay was observed first-hand, and no images were downloaded. UI observations are therefore mostly **INF** and are marked so.

Observed themes:
- Too many buttons.
- Top-edge buttons are a mobile problem.
- Buttons in the joystick zone can't be pressed.
- Oversized counters.
- Unclear icons.
- Cognitive overload.
- A UI per device.
