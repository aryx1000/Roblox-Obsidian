## MPL today
- 36 species, each defined by colour + material + descriptors, with no custom meshes recorded ([[Creature Overhaul#What exists today]]).
- Variants (Luminous, Chromatic, Prismatic) change appearance and value ([[Traits and Variants]]).
- The hatch reveal panel exists in My Lab.

## Recommended for MPL (candidates only)
1. **Silhouette before colour.** Group the 36 species into a few body plans with distinct silhouettes, then vary colour and material inside each. Biggest gap.
2. **Variants must read from 30 studs away.** Luminous = emits light, Chromatic = shifting colour, Prismatic = sparkle or particles. Like Neon in [[Adopt Me]].
3. **Mutation results show their parents:** one visible feature from each parent ([[Dragon Adventures]] principle).
4. **Hatch as a short event in the world** (egg wobble → crack → creature appears → walks to its pen), with the panel as a summary.
5. **Growth stages:** optional and scope-heavy. Note it, don't plan it yet.

## Creature acquisition feedback (pass 2)
How games make a new creature feel like an organism, not an inventory line (OBS/INF; sources in [[Research Sources#Pass 2: hatching and simulator systems (2026-10-15)]]):

| Pattern | Seen in | MPL suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Creature walks out and to its home | [[Steal a Brainrot]] (walks to base); Adopt Me! egg cracks in a nest (S41) | Walks from the incubator to its pen ([[Egg Hatching Research#Answers]], E) |
| Appears beside the player / follows | [[Adopt Me]], [[Pet Simulator 99]] | First look-at-player beat; optional "follow me" for a favourite |
| Camera focus | Sol's RNG cutscenes (S46) | ≤ 1 s gentle push, never a hard cut |
| Name / rarity banner | Ride A Pet promo card ([[Rarity Reveal Escalation]]) | Card: name → rarity colour → trait/variant chips |
| Idle / personality animation | Adopt Me! sleeping pet ([[Creature Silhouette and Personality]]); Bee Swarm "traits and personalities" (S39) | One idle-life per body plan; trait flavours the idle |
| First interaction | Adopt Me! needs | First feed of a new creature triggers a favourite-food reaction |
| Ownership + collection update | Index counts everywhere | Card "View in Index"; a silent count tick plus a milestone toast |

Saved visuals: [[Creature Silhouette and Personality]] (silhouette variety, sleeping idle, the shared-body-plan risk, size scale, customisation UI).

## What not to copy
- Meme characters or IP-based creatures.
- Pets sold for real money; trading without safeguards.
- 200+ species breadth. Go deep on 36 first.
