# Stud Shop Example

Back to [[UI Art Library]].

> [!warning] Not licensed for our games
> Mockup and reference use only. Before anything here ships, record its title, creator and source, and get the owner's OK on rights. Details: [[Figma to Roblox UI (ui-figma-roblox skill)#Licensing (important before a release)]].

The skill's worked example, Figma → Roblox `StarterGui`. The studs are baked into images because Roblox has no Overlay blend. The "user preferences" in this example belong to the skill author's client, not our owner. Scripts stay in the skill: `~/.claude/skills/ui-figma-roblox/examples/stud-shop/`.
