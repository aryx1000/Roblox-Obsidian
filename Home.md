## Starting a new game
1. Copy [[New Game]] into `Games/<Name>/<Name>.md`.
2. Read [[Working With the Owner]], [[Release and Publish Safety]] and [[Persistence and DataStores]] first. They hold the rules that are hardest to retrofit.
3. Set up backups on day 1 ([[Local Backups and Snapshots]]).
4. Log each work session in `Log/` using [[Session Log]].

## Log
Dated notes in `Log/` record what changed and what was learned. Promote durable lessons into `Knowledge/`.
